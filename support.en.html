FAQ

I bought "Unlock Everything" but things are still locked.
Tap "Unlock" in the top bar, then tap "Restore Purchases". You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If the purchase is waiting for approval (for example Ask to Buy), it unlocks automatically once approved.

Things are locked again after I changed iPhones or reinstalled.
Use "Restore Purchases". You will not be charged again.

What can I use for free?
Test 1 (center of gravity and stability), Test 2 (lift), and the distance event in "Take on records" after the tests (indoor arena, A4 copy paper; plane and crumpled ball, clips allowed) are free. Everything marked with a lock (🔒) — Tests 3 to 5, the Design Room, the flight-time and turning events, other paper materials and sizes, the Moon, Mars and the space station, and the cylinder — is included in "Unlock Everything", a one-time purchase.

I want a refund.
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My score does not appear on the global leaderboard (Game Center).
Sign in under Settings > Game Center on your iPhone. Only scores from "Take on records" thrown under the same fair conditions are sent: in the indoor arena, with no wind, no disturbance and A4 copy paper (plane or cylinder; clips are allowed). Throws made during the tests are never sent. Sending scores and viewing the leaderboard need a network connection.

Can I play offline?
Yes. Folding, flying and all the physics run on your iPhone. Only the global leaderboard and purchasing or restoring need a connection.

How do I delete my records?
Deleting the app removes all records and settings on that iPhone. Scores already on the Game Center leaderboards are kept by Apple's Game Center.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
