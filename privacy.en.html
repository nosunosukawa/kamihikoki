Kamihikoki Lab Privacy Policy

The app's screens are in Japanese.

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Kamihikoki Lab ("the app") does not collect your personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. All paper-airplane physics runs on your iPhone, and you do not need a network connection to play.

Information we collect

The developer does not receive any information about you. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The planes you made, your paper and throw settings, your personal bests and on-device rankings, and the event you last played
  • A note of whether you bought "Unlock Everything" (used only to show the right screen at launch; the actual purchase status is always checked with the App Store)

This data is stored only on your iPhone and never reaches the developer. It is removed when you delete the app. It may be included in your iPhone backups.

Game Center (global leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the app sends qualifying scores from "Take on records" (thrown in the indoor arena or on the hangar roof with no wind, no disturbance and A4 copy paper; throws made during the tests are never sent) to Apple's Game Center after each throw, and shows them on the leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible to other players on those leaderboards.

The app does not display, store or transmit your Game Center player ID. Game Center data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you do not want to take part, sign out in Settings > Game Center on your iPhone. Every other feature works while signed out.

Purchases ("Unlock Everything")

Purchases and payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app only checks whether your Apple Account owns "Unlock Everything". It never receives your payment information.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Parents can restrict Game Center features, such as interacting with other players, with Screen Time on the iPhone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting new information, this page will say so before that update is released.
